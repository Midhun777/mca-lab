About College

The College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) is a premier technical institution established in 1993 by the Government of Kerala and managed by the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD). Located in Alappuzha, Kerala, the college is approved by the AICTE and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). It is highly regarded for its undergraduate and postgraduate programs, particularly in Computer Science and Electronics, and was selected for the central government's TEQIP phase to upgrade its academic infrastructure. CEC boasts a strong placement cell that consistently connects graduates with top-tier global IT firms like TCS, Infosys, and IBM, while its accessible campus near the Chengannur railway station serves as a vibrant hub for student projects, technical festivals, and research.

Admission Process

Register & Exam: Apply for and appear in the respective entrance exam (KEAM for B.Tech, GATE for M.Tech, or MCA Entrance).Option Registration: Once results are out, register online on the CEE Kerala or LBS portal and add College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) as a top preference in your option list.Seat Allotment: Check the trial and final allotment rounds on the official portal.Physical Reporting: If allotted a seat at CEC, report to the college campus with your allotment memo, academic certificates, fee receipts, and identity proofs for physical verification and final enrollment.

Seat Distribution

The College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) provides an annual undergraduate B.Tech intake of 420 seats distributed across four branches, with 85% allocated via the KEAM Centralised Allotment Process and 15% reserved for the NRI quota. Additionally, a 10% supernumerary intake is available for direct second-year admissions through the Kerala Lateral Entry Test.
